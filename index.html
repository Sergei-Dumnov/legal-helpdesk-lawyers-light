About Peter O'Grady

Peter O'Grady trading as Legal Helpdesk Lawyers established the firm in 2014, bringing decades of dedicated legal experience to the community. Having continuously practiced law since 1996, Peter provides authoritative, practical, and empathetic legal counsel.

Credentials & Admissions

  • Academic: BA, LLB, Grad Cert Leg Prac.
  • Supreme Court: Admitted Solicitor of the Supreme Court of NSW.
  • High Court: Barrister and Solicitor of the High Court of Australia.
  • Specialization: Accredited Specialist in Personal Injury Law (2010–2016).
  • Notary: Appointed Notary Public.
